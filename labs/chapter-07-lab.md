# Chapter 7 Lab: Microsoft Purview: Compliance Foundations and Data Protection

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Conceptual exercise: decide whether a described scenario needs a sensitivity label, a DLP policy, or both, using the Microsoft Learn Purview information protection decision diagram

## Key takeaway

Sensitivity labels classify and protect content while DLP policies stop risky sharing — the exam expects you to distinguish between the two.
