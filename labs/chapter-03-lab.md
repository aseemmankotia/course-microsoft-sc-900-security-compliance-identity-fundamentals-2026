# Chapter 3 Lab: Microsoft Entra: Access Management and Identity Governance

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Conceptual exercise: design on paper a Conditional Access policy for 'block legacy authentication, require MFA outside the corporate network' using the Microsoft Learn Conditional Access policy diagram

## Key takeaway

Conditional Access enforces decisions while Identity Protection detects risk — governance tools like PIM and access reviews keep access time-bound and reviewed.
