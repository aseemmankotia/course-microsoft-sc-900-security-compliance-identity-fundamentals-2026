# Chapter 8 Lab: Microsoft Purview: Records, Insider Risk, eDiscovery, and Privacy

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Conceptual exercise: given a legal hold scenario, choose whether Content Search, eDiscovery Standard, or eDiscovery Premium is the appropriate tool, using the Microsoft Learn eDiscovery comparison table

## Key takeaway

Retention, eDiscovery, audit, and insider risk each address a distinct governance need — matching the described legal or risk scenario to the right tool is what the exam tests.
