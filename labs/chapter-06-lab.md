# Chapter 6 Lab: Threat Protection with Microsoft Defender XDR

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Conceptual matching exercise: given four described threats (phishing email, compromised on-prem AD account, risky SaaS app usage, malware on a laptop), map each to the correct Defender product

## Key takeaway

Each Defender for XDR component protects a distinct workload — endpoints, email, on-prem identity, or SaaS apps — and the exam tests correctly mapping threats to products.
